Questions.

          

            

              Is Tessera really free?
              
Yes. It’s open source under a free license. There are no paid tiers and no watermarks.

            

            

              What do I need to run it?
              
A computer from the last eight years with 8 GB of memory and a graphics card that supports OpenGL 4.3.

            

            

              Can I use my work commercially?
              
Yes. Everything you make belongs to you, including renders, models and animations.
